Just because we find a statistically significant difference/effect does not
necessarily indicate there is a causal relationship.
Because the hypothesis test relies on sample data, and because sample
data are not completely reliable, there is always the risk that misleading data will cause the hypothesis test to reach a wrong conclusion.


Power of a Hypothesis Test
The
power β of a hypothesis test is defined is the probability that the test will reject the null hypothesis when there is no effect.
The power of a test depends on a variety of factors including the size of
the effect and the size of the sample.
