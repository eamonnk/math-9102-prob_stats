However, because the model includes a **DO × temperature interaction**, this main effect represents the effect of dissolved oxygen on BOD when temperature is held at zero degrees Celsius. Consequently, the positive coefficient should not be interpreted as a simple unconditional relationship. The counter-intuitive direction of this coefficient, whereby higher dissolved oxygen is associated with higher BOD, may reflect the conditional nature of the effect and the role of dissolved oxygen in moderating the impact of temperature on microbial activity rather than acting as an independent linear predictor of water quality.
